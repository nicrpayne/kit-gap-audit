# Signal visual system v1 — draft

This is the durable product-facing specification. Detailed evidence, token
census, contradiction ledger, mockups, and migration notes live in
`artifacts/signal-visual-system-v1/`.

## Direction

Signal uses the Master Control Room’s tone as its primary visual reference:
graphite depth, warm-white type, restrained semantic light, compact precision,
and clear instrument physics. The Rubric-powered Audit World contributes its
world model, camera, evidence topology, disclosure, Trace, and selection
mechanics. Its orange/retro demo costume does not become Signal chrome.

Amber/orange is reserved for attention, unresolved state, staleness, and
warnings. It is not a generic accent.

## Palette contract

- Cyan: current/verified Reality and active truth.
- Violet: Scenario, hypothetical/candidate structure, and Decision structure.
- Amber: attention, unresolved, stale, needs action.
- Coral: risk, conflict, blocker, destructive action.
- Mint: accepted, available, favorable movement.
- Blue: source and provenance.
- Silver: evidence passage and neutral evidence/selection edge.
- Graphite: surface hierarchy, inactive structure, and comparison ghosts.

Hue never carries an entire truth distinction alone. Reality/Scenario,
current/stale/superseded, attested/inferred/external, and severity all add
text plus a glyph, shape, or stroke pattern.

The proposed **Signal Core** is conceptual only. The dedicated Reality Glyph
exploration branch owns the final center-glyph decision. This visual-system
track preserves the production Audit center and must not implement a competing
glyph. The current `S` monogram is also a placeholder, not a locked logo.

## Material contract

The established law remains: raised is operable; recessed is computed.
Persistent panels are opaque and crisp. Floating widgets are nearly opaque,
lightly edged, and use one ambient shadow. Gradients express depth within one
graphite family and never add decorative color. Chromatic glow must communicate
active selection, focus, or critical state.

## World and widget contract

Spatial instruments follow “World first. Widgets around the world.” Selection
opens a right-side Inspector while the world remains visible and operable.
Search, Overview, controls, Legend, and Trace use a common compact widget
family at the edges. Full review is a deliberate promotion from Inspector, not
the default response to selection.

For Audit, the current real Rubric-powered production world is the spatial
acceptance reference. The simplified prototype image demonstrates material and
widget placement only. Visual-system implementation must not modify, simplify,
redraw, reinterpret, or replace its geometry; Rings/Circle/Force/Hex; Project
World density; Source Systems horizon; Attention structure; Reality/Project
Model composition; fog/haze; node placement; camera; drag physics; morphs;
progressive identity; or relationship topology. The implementation is **same
Audit World + Signal visual language**.

The pattern is not forced onto every instrument. Portfolio retains dense
in-flow channels, Timeline retains its anchored temporal controls, Scope keeps
its composer, and Reports remains a reading surface.

## Portfolio / Capacity decision

Portfolio needs hierarchy rather than additional color: one dominant landing
outcome, a more authoritative shared field, a clear selected Scope, strongly
separated raised controls and recessed values, and semantic overlays for
availability/risk/attention. Per-Scope rainbow identity is retired. Density is
preserved.

## Implementation sequence

1. Add semantic tokens and compatibility aliases without restyling.
2. Add shared shell/widget/state/provenance primitives.
3. Converge Audit controls/widgets with Control Room material.
4. Improve Portfolio/Capacity hierarchy and material.
5. Migrate Decisions, Scope, Dependencies/Orbit, then Timeline.
6. Refine Forecast chart material only where still inconsistent.
7. Build future Reports directly on the new contract after upstream truth is
   ready.

## Status

The design system is **ready for implementation**. The audit identifies 18
current contradictions. Implementation should remain split by tranche because
`app/globals.css`, shared tool surfaces, Audit Inspector work, and Timeline SVG
materials are the principal merge/risk hotspots.
