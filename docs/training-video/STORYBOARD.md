- Record at 1440×900 or larger. Keep selected project and Reality/Scenario visible before narrating a number.
- Production establishing shots are read-only. All writes and exact transitions use the labeled disposable fixture.
- Rest the cursor during product laws. One click should have one narrated consequence.
- Do not crop coverage or currentness out of a Forecast shot.
- Fixture lower-third: `DISPOSABLE FIXTURE · production code 6c46ce9 · not production data`.

- Chapters 1–3: conceptual frames for 6–10 seconds.

- Always return to Reality before the final frame.
