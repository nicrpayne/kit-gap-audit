# Signal Showcase Script

**Target runtime:** 9:30–10:30.  
**Audience:** LinkedIn, portfolio, recruiters, hiring managers, product/delivery peers.  
**Data:** Disposable fixture, visibly labeled; production code `6c46ce9`.

## 1. Cold open — 0:00–0:45

**Screen:** Control Room. **Action:** Move across the five readings; no clicks.

“Most projects do not have an information shortage. They have an authority problem. The plan is in tickets, intent is in documents, decisions are in meetings, and the date is in somebody’s last status update.

I built Signal to answer a harder question: what do we currently accept as true about delivery, what remains unresolved, and what follows if something changes?

Signal is a governed delivery instrument. Changing Reality is deliberate. Calculating consequences is automatic.”

## 2. Second brain, not automatic truth — 0:45–1:35

**Screen:** Human Architecture visual. **Action:** Trace source → proposal → Reality.

“My knowledge environment—KE and Hermes—can reconstruct project history, connect evidence, and surface contradictions. But intelligence is not authority. A transcript passage is evidence. A wiki page is synthesis. Hermes can propose an implication. None of those should silently create Scope, staff a team, declare a dependency, or promise a date.

So Signal puts a human governance boundary in the middle. Knowledge proposes. Audit explains. The owning instrument accepts. Forecast recalculates.”

## 3. Audit and one governed change — 1:35–3:00

**Screen:** Audit world and fixture Change Inbox. **Action:** center → knowledge state → proposal → evidence → accepted outcome.

“Audit is the daily entry point. It compares current external intelligence and execution with accepted Reality. The world keeps source artifacts, passages, Findings, project structure, and Reality visible together. Search finds an object. Inspector explains it. Trace isolates the provenance path.

This widget tells me whether the latest completed knowledge and Signal snapshot agree. If Hermes is still ingesting, Signal waits. If the local companion is offline, Signal says so.

Here a new source proposes an Investigation capability. Before accepting, I see the current state, proposed owner mutation, project relevance, exact quote, source, and currentness. In this disposable fixture I accept it. Scope changes. The proposal moves into the processed audit trail. But no ticket mapping appears by magic, and the evidence does not become infallible. Audit discovers; Scope governs.”

## 4. An unresolved Decision — 3:00–4:00

**Screen:** Decisions circuit. **Action:** select ungated, then gated Decision; assume resolved; return.

“The same refresh revealed organizational tension. Signal represents that as an open Decision with two viable directions.

A Decision is not a gate. This open choice is real, but it has zero Forecast effect. Only when I can say what work waits, why it is serial, what evidence supports that claim, and how long resolution could take do I connect a DecisionGate.

I can assume that gate resolved in Scenario and see the modeled effect. Nothing is decided. Back to Reality restores the open gate.”

## 5. Capacity scenario and Forecast consequence — 4:00–6:25

**Screen:** Portfolio then Forecast. **Action:** show named exact; move one allocation; inspect impact; open Forecast.

“Capacity is part of the model. Signal distinguishes a legacy aggregate from a complete named roster. Named exact means the roster is confirmed and Forecast consumes the exact effective sum. It does not claim which ticket a person is working on.

Now I can ask a useful live question: what if one developer moves toward iTrack? The fader creates a disposable Scenario. Raw FTE is reduced by the shared context-switch assumption to effective FTE. The Inspector shows which project changed, which dependent project moved, and which project did not move because another constraint dominates.

In Forecast, I read coverage before the date. Forecastable means the execution model represents accepted shape and covered dependencies. Modeled subset means the math ran for incomplete inputs; Signal refuses to call that the project forecast.

The solid body is the Scenario. The dashed ghost is Reality. The center is likely; the span is the uncertainty window. The target line is evaluation. If I move the target, confidence changes but the distribution does not. Likely, target, and commitment are deliberately different things.

I discard the Scenario. An interesting answer does not become a staffing decision.”

## 6. Report — 6:25–7:45

**Screen:** Report Readiness, composer, finished brief. **Action:** select Delivery Leadership/Weekly Update; reveal history provenance.

“Before communicating, I check Report Readiness. Missing execution, stale knowledge, unreconciled Capacity, or incomplete Forecast becomes a named blocker with an owner door.

Then I choose an audience and purpose. Delivery Leadership needs outlook, movement, drivers, acceleration levers, asks, next milestones, source health, and caveats. An Executive version is shorter. The facts underneath are the same.

Creating the report freezes an immutable snapshot and recipe. When I reopen it, I can see when it was generated, whether it represents Reality or a Scenario, and how it differs from the live Forecast. Historical reports do not quietly update themselves.”

## 7. Project Activation — 7:45–9:05

**Screen:** Activation fixture sequence. **Action:** scan → provenance → manifest → First Audit; do not activate live.

“Signal can also start from dormant knowledge. Project Activation creates a pre-Reality identity, scans through the local companion, and produces a Bootstrap Review.

Every candidate stays NOT REALITY. Raw and derivative evidence are distinguished. Accept during review only means include in the manifest. The manifest says exactly what will become canonical and what will remain external or candidate.

In the disposable Harbor Relay example, activation creates the initial Scope, accepted capability and Decision, source registration, first frozen snapshot, and First Audit. It creates zero ticket links, gates, people, allocations, or milestones by implication. Forecast remains unavailable until executable work is truly mapped. That is a successful activation because it is honest.”

## 8. Close — 9:05–10:00

**Screen:** Signal at a Glance, then Control Room Reality baseline. **Action:** no cursor movement.

“What I find interesting about Signal is not that it produces another status view. It turns the relationship between knowledge, judgment, and consequence into something I can operate.

Before a meeting, I know what changed and what is stale. During a meeting, I can play a Scenario and explain the result. After the meeting, I can put new evidence upstream, govern the implications, and freeze a source-aware story for the right audience.

The knowledge system remembers. Audit discovers. Owner instruments govern. Forecast calculates. Reports communicate. Likely is not target; target is not commitment; and a candidate is never Reality until a human makes the accountable choice.

That is Signal: a way to play the project without pretending the project is simpler than it is.”
