# Signal Reality Glyph — design draft

- **Stage:** V1 direction finding
- **Scope:** Audit-local visual exploration
- **Decision owner:** Nic
- **Implementation status:** unmounted artifact only

## Meaning

Signal Audit needs a central glyph for **Reality**: the accepted state against
which evidence, intelligence, sources, and disagreement are located. It is not
a database, building, router, chip, or AI entity. It is the governed resolution
of many readings into one accepted reference.

The mark therefore needs to imply this transition:

> plurality → convergence → stable accepted point

## Visual posture

The preferred posture follows Master Control Room rather than Rubric's original
retro/orange voice: graphite ground, cyan signal, warm-white neutral, precise
geometry, quiet depth, and no ornamental futurism. The static silhouette must
carry the idea. A pulse or convergence animation may confirm state later, but
cannot be required for recognition.

## V1 recommendation

Advance these three to Nic review:

1. **Phase Lock** — clearest product philosophy and strongest 16 px behavior.
2. **Confluence** — strongest centered composition inside Audit World.
3. **Signal Fold** — most distinctive silhouette and best long-term mark
   potential, pending small-size refinement.

Do not advance **Quiet Core** or **Triangulate** without a compelling preference;
their broadcast/radar and network/crypto category associations are too common.
**Resolution Gate** is viable as a functional Audit icon, but less promising as
a broader Signal mark.

## Selection questions

- Should the mark feel directional, showing signals moving *toward* Reality
  (Phase Lock), or centered and omnidirectional (Confluence)?
- Should accepted Reality be a point/datum or a governed ring/boundary?
- Is the mark primarily an in-world center object, or should it carry equal
  weight as the left-rail and eventual product avatar?

The answers determine the V2 branch. No mounted production work should begin
until one or two directions are explicitly selected.
