# Evidence/currentness repair — 8 October 2026

Three defects reproduced by the independent staging browser acceptance task are repaired in this candidate. This is not whole-product acceptance or permission to publish a real-project forecast.

## Repairs

1. **Audit quote metadata.** Preserve explicit source dates, quote hashes, character offsets and their units through bootstrap activation, requirements, Findings, intelligence, the canonical graph, and View here. Retrieval/acceptance time never substitutes for the date of a conversation. Missing dates, source links, speakers and full transcript text remain missing rather than fabricated.
2. **Current knowledge versus history.** Current Audit Requirements and external Intelligence now use the materialized snapshot selected by the active package pointer. A changed source cannot leave an older package's head marked current. A/B/A restoration follows package identity, not creation order. Historical snapshots remain available to resolve the original citations of still-open Findings. Missing/racing materialization refuses currentness instead of reviving history.
3. **Accepted Scope evidence.** Bootstrap capabilities retain their top-level evidence references and show captured exact quotes from that Scope's immutable activation snapshot, with source date and original locators. They are explicitly labelled captured at acceptance, not current source state. Unknown/missing/foreign/non-bootstrap references remain reference-only. Packages are validated/indexed once per Scope request, not once per capability. No accepted row is rewritten by this read model.
