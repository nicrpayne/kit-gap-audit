# Delivery Leadership brief

This is the primary finished-report prototype.

The first viewport presents LIVE likely outcome/window, target, confidence, and immutable identity. It then keeps likely/target/commitment separate, shows historical movement, ranks two to four canonical drivers, exposes only Forecast-owned acceleration scenarios, separates operator-confirmed asks from candidates, compresses What Changed, and identifies What's Next.

Deterministic fixture result: likely Nov 1, 2026; window Oct 20–Nov 18; target Nov 15; confidence 78%; two days earlier than the previous saved brief; one gated decision carrying 1/3/6 serial days targeting Platform; no canonical delivery commitment.
