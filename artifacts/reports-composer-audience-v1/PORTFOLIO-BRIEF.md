# Portfolio / Staffing brief

The Portfolio preset promotes Capacity, executable Scope, delivery outlook, Forecast-owned scenarios, dependencies, Timeline, source health, and missing inputs.

When named allocations reconcile, it may show raw → effective → Forecast FTE and named contributors. When they do not, it shows `Named Capacity MISSING/UNAVAILABLE`, the Forecast FTE and source, and explicitly says this is not a named-staffing claim.

Allocation-by-project, support tail, and stranded/deferred-work fields are not present in the protected snapshot contract; this tranche does not infer them. See `samples/portfolio-missing-capacity.md`.
