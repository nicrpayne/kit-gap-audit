# Audience lenses

All lenses consume the same `DecisionBriefV1`. A lens is a communication preset, never a truth mode.

| Lens | Primary question | Default emphasis | Deliberately secondary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operator / PO | What changed and what needs work? | Audit delta, Decisions, dependencies, Capacity, evidence, source health | Executive compression |
| Delivery Leadership | Where are we landing, why, and what call helps? | Outlook, commitment distinction, movement, drivers, levers, promoted asks, next | Passage-level evidence detail |
| Executive | What outcome and call matter now? | Outcome, target, commitment absence/presence, deterministic read, confirmed asks | Work-item and contributor detail |
| Stakeholder / Partner | What changed and what should I expect next? | Outcome, movement, delivery change, next, declared dependencies, operator note | Internal evidence and Capacity details |
| Portfolio / Staffing | What delivery consequence follows from reconciled capacity? | Named/effective Capacity, executable Scope, scenarios, dependencies, source health | Unsupported allocation-by-project or support-tail claims |
| Decision / Scenario | What choice or canonical scenario changes the outcome? | Gates, target Scope, supported drivers, owner-provided scenario consequences, evidence | Broad status narration |

`Delivery Leadership / Weekly Update` is the recommended flagship. The audience selector intentionally does not contain a generic “People Manager” lens: without a first-class ownership/allocation contract, that label would imply authority and staffing knowledge Reports does not possess.
