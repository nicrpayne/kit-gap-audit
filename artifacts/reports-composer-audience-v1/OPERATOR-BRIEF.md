# Operator brief

The Operator / PO preset is the deepest mix: outcome, deterministic read, What Changed, supported drivers, every first-class open Decision, dependencies, executable Scope, reconciled Capacity, LIVE Timeline, Forecast-owned levers, Audit delta, evidence, source health, and caveats.

It preserves the accepted DecisionBriefV1 semantics while giving the content a modular hierarchy. Ungated open Decisions remain visible with zero modeled delay; gated Decisions carry canonical target Scope and range. Findings remain Audit observations and the explicit baseline-work boundary remains zero.
