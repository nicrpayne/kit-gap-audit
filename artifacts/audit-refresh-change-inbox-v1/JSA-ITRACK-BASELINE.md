## Cam/Colton law

Accepting `What investigation capability belongs in iTrack Rev 1?` creates one OPEN Decision containing both directions:

- Direction A: rebuild substantially as-is / minimal differences.

It selects neither direction and creates no DecisionGate. The contradiction is represented as unresolved organizational Reality, not discarded as bad data.
