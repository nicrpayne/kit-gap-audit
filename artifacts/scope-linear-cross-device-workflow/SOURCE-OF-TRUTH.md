# Source-of-truth contract

- **Hermes/wiki** = project knowledge and intelligence. It may support, contradict, or propose; it does not silently rewrite accepted product shape.
- **Signal Scope** = accepted product shape. Governed manual authoring and Audit acceptance write canonical owner rows.
- **Linear** = execution truth. Signal reads current work identity, state, estimates and hierarchy; it does not duplicate tickets as product definitions.
- **CapabilityWorkLink** = the explicit, operator-reviewed bridge between accepted shape and execution work.
- **Forecast** = a deterministic consequence of accepted shape, represented execution, estimates, capacity, gates and dependencies. The Monte Carlo engine remains protected.
- **Scenario** = a local hypothetical audition. It becomes Reality only through explicit governed commit.
- **Cross-device consistency** = all accepted Reality lives on the server; client caches are disposable reads and must revalidate.

The Scope Composer repeats the essential language at the moment of use: Scope owns product shape, Linear owns execution work, and linking is an explicit governed action.
