# Signal Reality Glyph — V1 exploration

Status: **ready for Nic review**, not selected, not a corporate logo.

This artifact explores one proposition:

> Many independent signals resolve into one accepted Reality.

It is deliberately isolated from the mounted product. No application route,
shared token, data contract, workflow, or Rubric runtime file is changed.

## Concepts

| # | Name | Core idea | Review read |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Phase Lock** | Three readings settle against one exact datum | Best overall; directional, restrained, legible, low category risk |
| 02 | **Confluence** | Four independent paths bend into one bounded center | Best Audit World center; balanced and immediately understandable |
| 03 | **Signal Fold** | Two differing waves cross phase and stabilize at one point | Most distinctive; strongest hero personality, slightly weaker at 16 px |

## Recommended shortlist

### 1. Phase Lock

The clearest embodiment of *many signals → one accepted Reality*. It does not
need a surrounding circle, survives 16 px, and feels like an instrument datum
rather than a generic AI badge. Its subtle asymmetry is useful: Audit gathers
evidence from the world, then resolves it at a deliberate acceptance boundary.

### 2. Confluence

The strongest centered/world mark. It reads equally well from every approach
and stays compact inside the existing Reality halo. The central ring preserves
the idea of a governed boundary. The main risk is resemblance to generic
focus/convergence symbols, so it would need a basic trademark similarity pass
before product-mark use.

### 3. Signal Fold

The most ownable silhouette in the set and the least dependent on familiar
target/network language. It communicates disagreement becoming coherence. Its
crossing is a little busy at 16 px, so a second geometry pass should tune the
optical join if selected.

## Construction rules

- One `64 × 64` viewBox.
- One `4` unit primary stroke with round caps and joins.
- Static form is canonical; motion is optional and never carries meaning.
- `currentColor` only, so every candidate works in cyan, warm white, or true
  one-color output.
- No words, letters, gradients, masks, raster dependencies, or proprietary
  source shapes inside the marks.
- Each standalone SVG includes `<title>`, `<desc>`, `role="img"`, and labelled
  semantics. Decorative embeddings in the prototype are hidden or labelled by
  their containing interface.

## Inspiration and similarity risks

These were drawn from the Signal proposition, not traced from third-party
marks. They still occupy familiar visual categories:

- **Phase Lock:** low risk. Possible adjacency to audio mixing, merge, and data
  normalization symbols; the terminal datum is the distinctive feature.
- **Confluence:** medium-low risk. Convergence/focus geometry is common, though
  the four bent feeds plus governed ring are less generic than a target.
- **Signal Fold:** low risk. Wave/interference language is common; this exact
  mirrored fold was created for this exploration and avoids an explicit `S`.
- **Resolution Gate:** medium risk. Could be read as merge/multiplexer/network
  routing. It is better suited to an Audit function icon than a company mark.
- **Quiet Core:** high risk. Radar, broadcast, Wi-Fi, and assistant marks often
  use concentric waves. Keep only as a useful control/comparison.
- **Triangulate:** high risk. Node-and-spoke and triangular networks are dense
  categories in AI, blockchain, security, and data products.

This is design screening, not legal clearance. Any concept promoted toward a
product or corporate mark should receive a trademark and visual-similarity
review across software, analytics, AI, and project-management categories.
