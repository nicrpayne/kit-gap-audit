# Signal Reality Glyph — focused optical refinement

Status: **four finalists ready for Nic review**. No production asset is mounted,
and no corporate logo is declared.

This pass stays inside the two selected V1 families:

- five optical Confluence variants;
- five optical Phase Lock variants;
- no Signal Fold work and no additional concept family.

## Outcome

### Best Confluence variants

1. **C3 · Four Gap** — the strongest Reality-center candidate. Four paths stop
   at a larger governed ring instead of crossing into a knot. The ring plus
   central point reads as one accepted Reality and holds excellent circular
   balance at 40, 48, and 64 px.
2. **C4 · Four Core** — the strongest alternate. Four paths stop before a solid
   accepted core, further reducing the four-way-knot association. It has more
   mark personality than C3 but gives up a little 16 px clarity.

The three-path tests were valuable negative evidence. **C1 Triad Datum** became
too close to share/network routing; **C2 Triad Radial** drifted toward propeller,
network, and crypto categories. Four paths are better for the Reality center,
provided their arrival is separated from the center boundary.

### Best Phase Lock variants

1. **P2 · Datum Ring** — the best Phase Lock refinement. Replacing the stop bar
   with a bounded point changes the destination from a wall into a governed
   accepted result while retaining excellent tiny-size recognition.
2. **P1 · Open Point** — the simplest and strongest at 16 px. It makes the result
   unambiguous, though the family remains more directional and closer to
   merge/routing language than Confluence.

**P3 Short Pulse** proves that shortening the bar is not enough; the destination
still reads as a stop. **P4 Quiet Datum** adds useful acceptance context but loses
crispness at tiny sizes. **P5 Weighted Settle** makes certainty visible but is too
busy for a primary mark.

## Product read

- **Reality center:** C3 is the lead, C4 the alternate.
- **Compact Signal/Audit utility:** P2 is the lead, P1 the alternate.
- **Broader Signal brand:** not decided here. Signal Fold remains parked for a
  future branch and was not modified.
