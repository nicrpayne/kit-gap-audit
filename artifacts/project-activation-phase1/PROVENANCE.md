# Provenance

The Inspector resolves the strongest supported chain:

`BootstrapCandidate → external current intelligence / typed claim → exact evidence passage → source artifact and stable locator`.

Evidence transported by an existing ContextSnapshot keeps its external ref, producer evidence id, structured data, offsets/hash metadata, and raw lineage roots. Signal Source/ContextDoc matches receive a stable internal ref plus an honestly labelled locator limitation because these stores have no character-offset index.

Wiki/source refs containing `wiki` or `ke://wiki` are labelled `SYNTHESIS · DERIVED`. Their passage independence is derivative and their raw lineage root, when known, is used for corroboration. Missing lineage remains unknown/zero; it never becomes independent by default.

Evidence attachment state is a review overlay. Detaching a citation leaves the immutable package passage untouched and appends a review event.
