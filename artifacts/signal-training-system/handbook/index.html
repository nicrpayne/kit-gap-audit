Product laws

Changing Reality is governed. Calculating consequences is automatic.
Audit discovers. Owner instruments govern.
Likely ≠ target ≠ committed.
Live owner ≠ fresh/current.
Candidate/proposal ≠ Reality.
Semantic relation ≠ dependency.
Person mention ≠ staffing.
Modeled subset ≠ full-project forecast.
